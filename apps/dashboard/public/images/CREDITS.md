# Photography

Three real photographs, all from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded into this folder rather than hotlinked. They are cropped and darkened in CSS only; the files themselves are untouched apart from a resize to 1800px at JPEG quality 76.

The CC BY-SA image's credit is rendered in the landing page footer, which is the attribution the licence asks for. Keep this file next to the images: a later session that swaps a photo should swap the credit with it.

Two candidates were downloaded and rejected. A "concrete architecture" file turned out to be a church interior, and a `Server_rack` lead image turned out to be a recording-studio console; running either would have let the page imply something about the subject that the photograph does not show.
